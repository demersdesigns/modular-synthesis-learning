## Functional Summary

### Generative / Ambient Power Tools
- **Pamela's PRO Workout** — master clock, euclidean rhythms, LFOs, probability, quantizer
- **Mimetic Digitalis** — 4-track CV sequencer, great for slow melodic drift
- **Bloom** — fractal/branching sequencer, probabilistic
- **Turing Machine Mk II** — random looping sequencer, classic generative tool
- **Pachinko** — stochastic sequencer
- **M185 Sequencer** — stage-based sequencer with repeats and resets
- **Pet Rock** — rogue-like daily-changing rhythm generator
- **Clep Diaz** — random/LFO/sequencer hybrid
- **uO_c** — 4 independent utilities (quantizer, S&H, logic, etc.)
- **Disting mk4** — swiss army knife utility
